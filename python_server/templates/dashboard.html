Plain English

This board practices with fake money so you can see whether the bot’s ideas actually make a dollar after fees — before any real wallet is used.

Total value — your practice account balance (cash + open trades).
Profit locked in — wins/losses from trades already sold.
Open gain/loss — paper profit if you closed open trades at the latest price.
Quick trade (scalp) — buy a coin, sell soon, aim for about $1 net after fees.
Price-gap trade (arb) — same coin priced differently in two places; paper “buy cheap / sell rich.”
Expected edge — forecast upside minus fees. Positive is hopeful; it still has to clear the ~$1 target.
Chart agree? (TA) — do RSI/MACD-style signals support the buy, or fight it?
Active hunt / Watch only — hunting tokens can be traded; watch-only are paused or cooling down.
Readiness score — checklist score for “is practice strong enough to consider real money?” Keep live trading off until it says ready.
Reset board — archive today’s logs and start a clean practice score from $1000 again.
Fees / slip — trading isn’t free: pool fees plus price moving while you buy/sell. The bot subtracts these before calling something a good idea.
Pipeline — the step-by-step checks (safety → forecast → costs → risk → paper buy/sell).
